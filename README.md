# hotel
The Hotel Executive Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard developed to analyze hotel revenue, booking performance, guest numbers, average length of stay, and revenue

 
** Project Objectives**

1.Monitor total hotel revenue and booking performance.

2.Analyze revenue generated by different room types.

3.Track monthly revenue trends and identify peak and low-performing periods.

4.Monitor the total number of guests and average length of stay.

5.Support data-driven decisions related to pricing, marketing, and room management.

6.Provide an interactive dashboard for executive-level performance monitoring.

Key Insights

1. Overall Hotel Performance

The hotel recorded approximately 702M in total revenue, around 1,000 bookings, and 245 guests. The average stay is 5.56, which may indicate opportunities to develop packages that encourage longer stays.

2. Revenue by Room Type

The Standard room type generates the highest revenue at approximately 0.67bn, followed by:

Executive: 0.63bn

Deluxe: 0.60bn

Suite: 0.59bn

Standard rooms are the leading room type by revenue in the displayed chart, while Suite rooms generate the lowest revenue among the four categories.

3. Monthly Revenue Analysis

The monthly revenue chart shows noticeable fluctuations throughout the year.

February: The highest revenue point, at approximately 95M.

July: Revenue drops sharply to approximately 35M.

October: The lowest revenue point, at approximately 25M.

November–December: Revenue recovers toward the end of the year.

These changes may reflect seasonal demand, pricing differences, marketing activity, or changes in booking volume. Further analysis is needed to establish the underlying causes.

4. Revenue Distribution Across Room Types

Revenue is relatively close across the four room categories, ranging from approximately 0.59bn to 0.67bn in the displayed chart. This suggests that revenue is not concentrated in only one room category.

However, the figures shown in the room-type chart appear inconsistent with the 702M total revenue KPI. The aggregation level, currency units, filters, and measures should be checked before making final comparisons.

5. Interactive Filtering

The Month, Year, and Room Type slicers allow users to explore performance for specific periods and room categories. This helps management investigate changes in revenue and compare room-type performance under different conditions.

**Recommendations**

1.Improve low-season performance: Introduce targeted promotions, seasonal discounts, and attractive accommodation packages during low-revenue months such as October and July.

2.Capitalize on peak periods: Use the high revenue recorded in February to investigate demand drivers and prepare pricing and staffing strategies for similar periods.

3.Optimize room pricing: Review room rates, occupancy levels, and operating costs to improve revenue and profitability across all room types.

4.Promote longer stays: Offer weekly packages, loyalty rewards, and discounts for extended stays where profitable.

5.Investigate revenue fluctuations: Compare monthly bookings, room occupancy, average daily rates, cancellations, and customer segments to identify the reasons behind revenue changes.

6.Strengthen marketing strategies: Use historical booking patterns to target customers during months with lower demand.

7.Improve KPI consistency: Validate the total revenue measure against revenue by room type and month to ensure that all visuals use consistent filters, units, and aggregation logic.
